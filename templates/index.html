          Clinical Workflow

          How LankaMedi AI Works

          A 4-step clinical pipeline built for patient safety, privacy, and clinical accuracy.

          📄

          1. Multi-Format Upload

          Upload photographed lab slips, scanned PDFs, or paste medical text directly. Automatic image enhancement ensures high OCR legibility.

          🛡️

          2. PII / NIC Redaction

          Before sending data anywhere, Sri Lankan National Identity Card (NIC) numbers, telephone numbers, emails, and patient names are stripped.

          🔬

          3. Biomarker Range Match

          Values (Platelets, FBS, HbA1c, Creatinine, Cholesterol) are evaluated against established Sri Lanka Medical Association (SLMA) intervals.

          🤖

          4. Trilingual AI Synthesis

          Advanced AI models (NVIDIA NIM & Google Gemini) synthesize personalized summaries in English, Sinhala, or Tamil with 1990 triage.
